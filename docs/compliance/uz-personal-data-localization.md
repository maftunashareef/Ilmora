# Uzbekistan personal-data law and ILMORA hosting: localization, registration, cross-border flows

Research date: 2026-10-18. Status: research note, NOT legal advice and NOT a compliance statement.
Scope: ILMORA processes names, phone numbers, attendance and payment records of Uzbek residents
(students, often minors; parents; teachers; centre staff).

Headline (confirmed from lex.uz): the blanket "store all citizens' data in Uzbekistan" rule of 2021
(Art. 27-1) was replaced on 27.03.2026 by Law ZRU-1125. Mandatory in-country storage now covers only
biometric data, genetic data and telecom-operator subscriber data; other personal data may be stored and
processed abroad if one of three conditions is met (adequate country / approved standard clauses or
binding corporate rules / approved international standards). State Register registration (Art. 20) is now
tied to the same three categories. A 49-country adequacy list was approved on 29.07.2026 (Res. 415).
The standard contractual clauses and the international-standards list had NOT been found as adopted acts
as of the research date.

## 2. What the law requires (article numbers as seen in the sources)

Terminology (P1 Art. 4; Uzbek per P2): personal data = information on any medium relating to an identified
or identifiable natural person (shaxsga doir ma'lumotlar); database = a database in the form of an
information system containing personal data (shaxsga doir ma'lumotlar bazasi); processing (ishlov berish)
= any one or combination of: collection, systematization, storage, change, supplementation, use,
provision, distribution, transfer, anonymization, destruction; operator / owner (operator / mulkdor);
third party (uchinchi shaxs). ILMORA is an operator and (for its own platform database) an owner; the
education centres using it are also owners/operators of their students' data (role split: inferred).

2.1 Localization (P1/P3 Art. 27-1 "Special conditions for storage and processing", in force 27.03.2026)
- Part 1: personal data of citizens of Uzbekistan must be stored while ensuring the basic principles of the Law.
- Part 2: mandatory storage on the territory of Uzbekistan for: (a) biometric data of individuals, (b) genetic
  data of individuals, (c) data of individuals who are users of services of telecommunications operators
  operating in Uzbekistan.
- Part 3: personal data not listed in part 2 "may be stored and processed outside the territory of the
  Republic of Uzbekistan" if ONE of these holds: (i) the foreign state is recognized as ensuring adequate
  protection of personal data; (ii) the operator has adopted and complies with standard contractual clauses
  or binding corporate rules meeting requirements approved by the authorized state body; (iii) the operator
  complies with international standards for personal-data management and storage from a list approved by
  the authorized state body.
- Part 4: the adequacy list is set by the Cabinet of Ministers -> Res. 415 (P5), 49 states/territories incl.
  all EU members, UK, Switzerland, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Israel, Japan, South Korea, Singapore,
  Hong Kong, Canada, Argentina, Brazil, Uruguay, New Zealand, Russia, Guernsey/Jersey/Isle of Man, Faroe
  Islands, Andorra, and the USA "only for companies operating within the EU-US Data Privacy Framework".
- Historical (P4 Art. 13, in force mid-April 2021 to 26.03.2026): the owner/operator processing citizens' data
  with IT "including the Internet" had to ensure collection, systematization and storage "in personal-data
  databases on technical means physically located in Uzbekistan and registered in the State Register".
  ZRU-1125 has no transitional provisions (P3 Art. 4: in force on publication).

2.2 Cross-border transfer (P1 Art. 15, unchanged by ZRU-1125)
- Transfer = transfer by owner/operator outside Uzbekistan. Allowed to states ensuring adequate protection
  of data-subject rights. To other states only with: the subject's consent to the cross-border transfer;
  necessity to protect the constitutional order, public order, rights and freedoms, health and morality; or
  under international treaties. Transfer may be prohibited or restricted on listed public-interest grounds.
- Res. 415 (P5) para 4: transfers to listed states "through information systems created on the basis of
  international agreements" go automatically, without extra permits or notifying the authorized body
  (wording appears aimed at inter-state systems; applicability to private SaaS transfers is unclear);
  transfer "in any form" to NON-listed states is allowed only when the legal, organizational and technical
  conditions set by the authorized body are met; if a leak is detected "in the process of cross-border
  transfer" the operator must notify the authorized body within 24 hours and give detailed information on
  causes and remediation within 72 hours. Para 7: the MIA with other agencies must approve the requirements
  for standard contractual clauses and binding corporate rules within three months (i.e. by about
  29.10.2026) - not found as adopted on 2026-10-18. Para 6: Uzbekistan to seek accession to Council of
  Europe Convention 108.
- Art. 22 (P1): the data subject may request information about "performed or intended cross-border transfer".

2.3 Registration in the State Register (P1 Art. 20 as amended by ZRU-1125; P6; P11)
- Art. 20 part 1 (new): databases "subject to mandatory storage on the territory of Uzbekistan under part 2
  of Art. 27-1" must be registered in the State Register of personal-data databases. Part 2: registration is
  by notification, application to the authorized body. Part 3 exemptions (members of NGOs/religious bodies;
  data made public by the subject; name-only databases; one-time entry passes; state automated systems;
  non-automated processing; data processed under labour law). Part 4: notify the authorized body of any
  change to registration data within 10 calendar days.
- Art. 31 (P1) still lists among operator duties "register the databases owned and/or processed" - read
  together with Art. 20 this appears to apply only to registrable databases (inferred).
- Procedure (P6, as amended 24.12.2025): applicant = owner and/or operator; via Public Service Centres or
  the Unified Portal of interactive state services (EPIGU); free of charge (para 9); the authorized body
  registers or refuses within five working days of receiving the form (para 10); refusal only for inaccurate
  or incomplete information (para 11); changes must be filed within seven working days (para 15; note the
  Law says 10 calendar days). Form fields include: database name, start date, termination condition,
  COUNTRY and address where the database is located, purpose, whether managed remotely, whether cross-border
  transfer is possible, responsible person, list of data categories (name, birth date, PINFL, photo,
  education institutions, address, phone, etc.). pd.gov.uz FAQ: login via OneID (id.egov.uz); certificate is
  issued electronically to the personal cabinet (P11).
- Authorized body: Law text (Art. 8) still names the "State Center for Personalization under the Cabinet of
  Ministers"; Res. 71 (2025 wording), Res. 415 para 3 and pd.gov.uz now name the "Department of Migration
  and Personalization under the Ministry of Internal Affairs" (Uzbek: Ichki ishlar vazirligi huzuridagi
  Migratsiya va personallashtirish departamenti), created by PP-14 (P10). DLA Piper (S1) records an
  intermediate "Personalization Agency under the Ministry of Justice" from 01.01.2023. Art. 8 powers include
  maintaining the Register, issuing registration certificates, state control, and binding orders
  (predpisaniya) to eliminate violations. Contact per P11: Tashkent, Bog'ishamol 160a; pdpgovuz@gmail.com;
  +99871 207 70 64 (registration).

2.4 Lawful basis, consent, purpose (P1)
- Art. 18: processing allowed on consent; necessity for a contract with the subject (or pre-contract steps
  at the subject's request); legal obligations of the owner/operator; protection of legitimate interests;
  legitimate interests of owner/operator/third party or socially significant goals if subjects' rights are
  not violated; statistics/research with mandatory anonymization; publicly available sources.
- Art. 19: purposes must be defined in the operator's regulatory/founding documents, must match purposes
  declared at collection; new purpose requires new consent; data must be accurate; scope must match purposes.
- Art. 21: consent in any form that allows confirming it was obtained; WRITTEN consent (incl. electronic
  document) for special personal data; for MINORS consent is given in written form (incl. electronic
  document) by parents/guardians; withdrawal in the same form or in writing.
- Art. 23: at inclusion into a database the subject must be notified in writing of the purposes and of the
  Art. 30 rights; on transfer to a third party the owner/operator notifies the subject in writing within three
  days (exceptions listed, e.g. state bodies, statistical/scientific purposes).
- Art. 22: subject's right to information (confirmation, grounds and purposes, methods, operator identity
  and persons with access, data composition and source, retention periods, cross-border transfer).

2.5 Operator duties and security (P1)
- Art. 27: owner/operator and third parties take legal, organizational and technical measures ensuring
  integrity, confidentiality and prevention of unlawful processing. Art. 28: confidentiality - no disclosure
  or distribution without consent or another legal ground.
- Art. 31: comply with the law; respond to subjects; approve the necessary and sufficient data composition;
  destroy data when the purpose is achieved; prove consent; correct/destroy inaccurate data; suspend
  processing on signs of violation; accept electronic requests; notify subjects of changes/destruction/
  restriction and of transfers to third parties; register databases; take protection measures. Processing may
  be entrusted to a third party with the subject's written consent, in execution of a contract with the
  subject, or where provided by law. A structural unit or official responsible for processing/protection
  must be designated and work under the Model Procedure (LexUz annotation cites Ministry of Justice orders
  No. 19-mx of 14.11.2023 and No. 20-mx of 15.11.2023 - not read).
- Breach notification: no general duty found in the Law (consistent with S1); the only confirmed duty is
  Res. 415 para 4 (leak during cross-border transfer, 24 h / 72 h). pd.gov.uz offers a breach-report form.

2.6 Liability (P7, P8, P9)
- Admin Code Art. 46-2 part 1: unlawful collection/.../destruction of personal data, "as well as
  non-compliance, when processing citizens' personal data using IT incl. the Internet, with the requirements
  to collect, systematize and store personal data on technical means physically located in Uzbekistan and in
  databases registered in the State Register" - fine 7 BRV (citizens) / 50 BRV (officials). Part 2 (2026):
  unlawful processing using AI technologies and its dissemination - 50 to 100 BRV with confiscation.
  NOTE: part 1 still mirrors the 2021 localization wording although Art. 27-1 changed in 2026 (inferred risk
  of inconsistent enforcement). pd.gov.uz FAQ quotes older, lower amounts - lex.uz text prevails.
- Criminal Code Art. 141-2: the same acts committed after an administrative penalty - fine 100 to 150 BRV,
  or deprivation of a right up to 3 years, or correctional labour up to 2 years; aggravated (group,
  repeat, mercenary motives, official position, grave consequences) - 150 to 200 BRV, correctional labour
  2 to 3 years, restriction of liberty 1 to 3 years, or imprisonment up to 3 years.
- Law on Informatization Art. 12-1 part 3: access to a website may be restricted by the specially authorized
  body under a Cabinet procedure when the owner breaches the duties in parts 1-2; part 1 now includes
  (ZRU-1115, 2026) preventing unlawful AI-based processing of personal data. The 2021-era blocking ground
  for localization non-compliance (reported by S1 and a 2023 news item on pd.gov.uz) was NOT found in the
  current text - historic versions could not be retrieved.
- Art. 33 of the Law refers to liability "in the established manner"; LexUz annotation points to 46-2 and 141-2.

## 3. What this means for ILMORA's architecture

Working assumption (inferred, see open questions 1-2): ILMORA data (names, phones, attendance, payments,
QR check-ins without face recognition) is ordinary personal data, not biometric/genetic and not
telecom-operator subscriber data, so it is NOT subject to mandatory in-country storage and NOT subject to
mandatory State Register registration under the 2026 wording. Consent/notification/security duties apply
regardless of where data sits. Payment data is not "special" data under Art. 25 (list seen in P1 summary).

## 4. UNVERIFIED / open legal questions

1. Scope of "data of users of telecommunications operators' services" (Art. 27-1 part 2 c): plain reading
   is telecom operators' own subscriber data, but a dataset of phone numbers held by a non-telecom could be
   argued to fall inside. No official interpretation found.
2. Whether a business may (or should) register a non-registrable database voluntarily after 27.03.2026, and
   whether databases registered under the 2021 regime remain in the Register; ZRU-1125 has no transitional
   provisions; registration remains free and takes five working days (P6).
3. Whether anonymised/scrubbed Sentry traces sent abroad count as personal-data processing abroad; depends on
   residual identifiers (IP, user id) and on whether IP addresses are personal data under Art. 4 (untested).
4. Whether encrypted off-site backups (provider has no key) are "storage outside the territory" under
   Art. 27-1 part 3; the Law has no pseudonymization concept; an adequate-list destination is the only clearly
   confirmed route.
5. Interplay of Art. 15 (transfer to non-adequate states possible with the subject's consent) and Art. 27-1
   part 3 (three conditions, consent not among them); S5 raises the same conflict.
6. Practical compliance with Art. 23 written notification "within three days" of each transfer to a third
   party (SMS gateway, payment provider) when the notice is given up-front in the terms.
7. Status of the standard contractual clauses / binding corporate rules requirements and the international
   standards list (Art. 27-1 part 3 ii-iii; Res. 415 para 7 deadline about 29.10.2026): not found as adopted.
8. Whether Res. 415 para 4 (automatic transfer via "information systems created under international
   agreements") adds any condition for private transfers to listed states, and which US vendors (GitHub,
   Sentry, AWS, Twilio) are actually inside the EU-US DPF.
9. Admin Code 46-2 part 1 still describes the pre-2026 localization duty; how enforcement treats data stored
   abroad under Art. 27-1 part 3 is untested.
10. Whether the Law's blocking mechanism of 2021 (website access restriction for localization breaches) still
    exists in any act; current Art. 12-1 of the Informatization Law contains only the AI-processing ground.
11. Exact BRV value in 2026 (not taken from a primary source; S6 implies about 412,000 UZS per BRV).
12. Content of ZRU-1174 (12.09.2026, Art. 25 special data) - lex.uz Russian translation pending; not read.
13. Whether a profile photo used only for display (no recognition) is "biometric data" (Art. 26 not read
    in full); face-recognition attendance would trigger mandatory localization and registration.

## 5. Asoschi uchun qisqa xulosa (o'zbek, lotin)

2026-yil 27-martdan boshlab (O'RQ-1125) "fuqarolarning barcha shaxsga doir ma'lumotlari faqat
O'zbekistondagi serverlarda saqlansin" degan qat'iy talab bekor bo'ldi: endi 27-1-modda bo'yicha faqat
biometrik, genetik va telekom-operator abonentlari ma'lumotlari O'zbekistonda majburiy saqlanadi, Davlat
reyestrida ro'yxatdan o'tkazish (20-modda) ham shu uch turdagi bazalarga tegishli. ILMORA ism, telefon,
davomat va to'lov yozuvlarini qayta ishlaydi - bu oddiy shaxsga doir ma'lumotlar, shuning uchun (bizning
talqinimizcha, yurist tasdig'i kerak) majburiy lokalizatsiya va ro'yxat talab qilinmaydi. Shunga qaramay
asosiy bazani Toshkentdagi VPS'da saqlash eng xavfsiz yo'l; chet elga chiqadigan har qanday oqim (zaxira
nusxalar, S3, Sentry, loglar) faqat Vazirlar Mahkamasining 415-son qarori (29.07.2026) ro'yxatidagi
davlatlarga (masalan, Germaniya, Niderlandiya; AQSh - faqat EU-US DPF'dagi kompaniyalar) yo'naltirilishi
yoki shaxsiy ma'lumotlardan tozalanishi kerak. Rozilik (voyaga yetmaganlar uchun ota-onaning yozma, shu
jumladan elektron roziligi - 21-modda), maqsadni cheklash (19-modda), yozma xabarnoma (23-modda), maxfiylik
va himoya choralari (27, 28, 31-moddalar), mas'ul shaxs tayinlash - joylashuvdan qat'i nazar majburiy.
Javobgarlik: MJtK 46-2-modda (7/50 BHM jarima), JK 141-2-modda (takroriy holatda jinoiy javobgarlik).
Vakolatli organ hozir IIV huzuridagi Migratsiya va personallashtirish departamenti (pd.gov.uz). Standart
shartnoma shartlari va xalqaro standartlar ro'yxati hali tasdiqlanmagan - 4-bo'limdagi ochiq savollarni
yurist bilan ko'rib chiqish zarur. Bu hujjat yuridik maslahat emas.
